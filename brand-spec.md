# Re:CombineControl Brand Spec

## Design Read

- Artifact: one-page landing page
- Audience: HL2RP players, former CombineControl players, prospective staff, and genre veterans
- Visual language: editorial dystopian control-room, with sharp typography and restrained systems UI
- Mode: redesign-overhaul of a placeholder Vite app
- Visual variance: 7
- Motion intensity: 4
- Information density: 5
- Asset dependence: 3
- Brand fidelity: 2

## Design Decisions

- Palette:
  - Background: `#070707`
  - Surface: `#101010`
  - Text strong: `#f2efe8`
  - Text body: `#d5d0c7`
  - Text muted: `#8b857c`
  - Accent: `#ce3023`
- Typography:
  - Display: `Barlow Condensed`
  - Body: `IBM Plex Sans`
  - Utility: `IBM Plex Mono`
- Spacing system: 8px base with large sectional jumps
- Shape language: sharp corners, minimal rounding, strong borders
- Motion style: short hover lifts and state transitions only

## Asset Notes

- The old `logo.png` was reviewed for tone and typographic inspiration only.
- The previous wordmark is not reused as an identity asset on the site.
- The current page relies on typography, composition, and color rather than imported brand imagery.
